About me

I started my journey in visual communication design, where I learned to craft compelling narratives through visuals. But I wanted to go beyond aesthetics—to design experiences that truly connect with people. That's why I pursued interaction design, blending my visual expertise with user-centered problem-solving.

Through freelancing with entrepreneurs, I helped bring their ideas to life, and as a tutor, I gained a deeper appreciation for diverse perspectives. Additionally, working on industry-academic collaboration projects exposed me to different disciplines and problem-solving approaches, further broadening my perspective on design and innovation.
‍
Collaboration, attention to detail, and a commitment to positive change guide my work. My dream is to build communities where people can share, support one another, and find meaningful connections. Through my designs, I strive to create experiences that not only solve problems but also bring people together, fostering a more connected and compassionate society.

Illustration

I love drawing and illustration — it's something I pursue purely for joy. Whether it's quick sketches or more detailed pieces, creating illustrations is how I recharge and explore ideas outside of structured design work.
