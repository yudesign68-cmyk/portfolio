Background

When I was randomly assigned the theme of "luxury" for this conceptual dashboard project, I took a moment to redefine what luxury means to me. Rather than associating it with expensive goods or extravagant lifestyles, I interpreted luxury as having the time and space to slow down—to feel calm, present, and at ease. In that sense, reading felt like the perfect match: it's a quiet indulgence, a moment of pause, and a personal escape from the everyday.

With this idea, I designed a Thematic Reading Dashboard that captures the feeling of timeless relaxation through a soft, elegant visual language—especially inspired by romantic novels. The goal was to create an experience that feels not only visually refined but also personal and functional.

As a sole designer on this project, I focused on creating a fully responsive dashboard that blends both quantitative data (like reading habits and progress tracking) and qualitative data (such as book preferences and saved quotes). The result is a customisable, immersive interface that invites users to engage more meaningfully with their reading journeys.
